        How this ranking is computed

        The dashboard keeps a small min-heap of the K strongest trips while it scans the matching trips once. It never sorts the whole list. Trips with a missing fare or distance are excluded from the ranking. Trips under 0.5 miles are left out of the trend line only.

        06 Methodology

        Data and method

        Source
        NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission (TLC) Yellow Taxi Trip Records, with taxi zone names from the TLC taxi zone lookup.
        processing
        Trips are cleaned and prepared by the project data pipeline. Trips with a missing fare or distance are excluded from the outlier ranking but remain in the table.
        Coverage
        The period shown in city pulse is the period of the data currently loaded.
        Development data
        While this page reads mock/mock_data.json, every number is a development placeholder, not a finding about New York. It is replaced by processed data from the project API.
